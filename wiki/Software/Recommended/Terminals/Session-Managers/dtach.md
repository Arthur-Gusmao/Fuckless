## dtach

A tiny program that emulates the detach feature of screen.

dtach exists for one purpose: allowing a terminal session to detach and later reattach.

### Minimal scope

It intentionally does not implement terminal multiplexing, layouts, scripting, or window management.

### A composable piece

dtach is one of the simplest examples of the Unix philosophy.

Its entire purpose is to provide a single feature that can be combined with other tools.
