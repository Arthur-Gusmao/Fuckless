## Unix Tools

The basic Unix tools are often more powerful than any text editor.

cat, cp, sed, awk, grep, diff, cut, tr, and ed each do one thing. Piped together, they do whatever you need.

### Small pieces, powerful chains

An editor is one program that must anticipate every editing task. The Unix tools make no such promise.

You filter, transform, and combine with a chain of small programs. Each step is replaceable, testable, and readable.

### ed over everything

Among these tools, ed stands alone.

It is the original text editor and the grandfather of everything this wiki recommends. Every vi, sam, and sed line editor inherits from it.

### Tools before editors

Reach for the tools first.

They compose with everything, never get in the way of the terminal, and never try to be an application.
