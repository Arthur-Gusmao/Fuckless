## OpenBSD

The operating system where the dragons live.

OpenBSD is a small, secure BSD that values correctness, simplicity, and free documentation.

### Security as a method

Proactive auditing, privilege separation, and "secure by default" are the norm, not features.

### Small and coherent

The whole base system is a single tree: kernel, userland, and X build together.

### The codebase is the manual

Every component is small enough to read, and the docs are honest.

The sanest Unix for people who like the whole system to make sense.
