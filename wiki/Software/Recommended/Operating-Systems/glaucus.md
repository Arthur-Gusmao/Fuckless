## glaucus

An opinionated Linux distribution based on musl libc and toybox.

glaucus replaces every piece of GNU software with a simpler alternative.

### musl and toybox

musl for libc, toybox for core utilities, and kinit as the init.

### No GNU, no systemd

Every component is replaced by something small you can read.

### Opinionated on purpose

Its philosophy is explicit: simplicity and freedom from the GNU stack are features, not constraints.
