## iglunix

A Linux distribution with no GNU components at all.

iglunix is a from-scratch experiment that drops every piece of GNU software from the base system.

### Fully non-GNU

No glibc, no GCC, no coreutils: the toolchain, init, and utilities are built independently.

### Multi-kernel

The userland is portable and targets Linux and other kernels, not just one base.

### Opinionated and small

Its own package manager and init, with a base so small you can read it.

An extreme, honest answer to the question of how little GNU a Linux needs.
