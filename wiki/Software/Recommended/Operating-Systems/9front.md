## 9front

A fork of the Plan 9 operating system.

9front continues Plan 9's research heritage with active development and modern hardware support.

### The Plan 9 way

Everything is a file, one namespace per process, no Unix security model.

### A single system

The kernel, userland, and window system (`rio`) are one coherent whole, built from a single tree.

### The answer to "what next?"

Plan 9 proper is frozen history; 9front is where the ideas live on and keep evolving.

The most faithful operating system for the philosophy behind this wiki.
