## slgro

A floating, keyboard-driven compositor for neuswc.

slgro focuses on controlling windows from the keyboard.

Moving, resizing, and centering windows are all bound to keys, turning the compositor into something closer to a text interface for your screen.

### Keyboard-driven

Windows are manipulated through keybindings rather than a pointer.

For users who live in a tiling mindset but prefer floating windows, slgro offers precision without reaching for the mouse.

### Lua configuration

Configuration is written in Lua, a small scripting language with a tiny implementation.

It provides the flexibility of a configuration language without dragging in a heavyweight runtime, and the config stays readable and explicit.
