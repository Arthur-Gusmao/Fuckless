## neuwm

A Wayland compositor based on neuswc, forked from wsxwm and inspired by hevel.

neuwm combines two lineages.

From wsxwm it inherits a tiling foundation; from hevel it takes Plan 9-inspired ideas about how windows should behave.

### Two inspirations

Tiling gives structure to the screen without manual arrangement.

Plan 9 ideas remind the user that the compositor should stay out of the way. neuwm tries to keep the useful parts of both without the weight of either.

### Built on neuswc

Like the rest of the neuswc family, neuwm is a small program on a small library.

There is no web view, no plugin runtime, no settings portal. What you read is what runs.
