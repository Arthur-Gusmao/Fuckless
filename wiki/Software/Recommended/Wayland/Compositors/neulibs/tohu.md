## tohu

A simple floating compositor built on neuswc.

tohu is a floating compositor that does not pretend to be a desktop environment.

Windows are placed and arranged by the user, freeform, without a tiling algorithm deciding where they go.

### Floating, not tiling

Some users want their windows where they left them.

tohu gives the classic floating model its own compositor: no dynamic layouts, no automatic arrangement, just windows on a screen that respond to input.

### Fewer rules

Floating is the honest model for certain workflows.

It removes the machinery of layout management entirely, and what is removed cannot misbehave.

### The neuswc family

tohu is part of the neuswc ecosystem of compositors, sharing a small, hackable foundation.

A fork called mot exists as well, proof that these tools are made to be taken apart and rebuilt.
