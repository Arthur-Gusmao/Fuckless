## dwc

A dynamic Wayland compositor based on neuswc.

dwc brings dynamic window management to Wayland.

In the tradition of dwm and dvtm, the layout adapts automatically as windows open and close, without the user arranging anything by hand.

### Dynamic layouts

The compositor decides how windows share the screen, and that arrangement updates itself as windows appear and disappear.

No drag-to-resize, no floating window ballet. The layout is a consequence of the work, not a task in itself.

### The dwm spirit

dwc follows the design that made dwm a legend in minimal computing.

A small codebase, predictable behavior, and a focus on the few things that matter, leaving the rest to other programs.
