## ropotamo

A StumpWM-like tiling window manager for river, written in Janet.

ropotamo manages windows as a numbered list, in the spirit of ratpoison and StumpWM.

### StumpWM heritage

Windows live on a screen and are reached by number and key, not by pointer.

Like StumpWM, it is built on a Lisp-family language, here Janet.

### Keyboard-driven

Everything is reachable from the keyboard: no title bars, no mouse habits, no decorations.

### Small

A tiling window manager in the StumpWM tradition is small by design, and ropotamo stays that way.
