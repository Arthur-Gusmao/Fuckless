## orilla

A dynamic tiling window manager for river, written in Rust.

orilla takes its inspiration from XMonad.

### XMonad-inspired

Layouts are arranged in the XMonad spirit: a small set of composable tiling schemes driven entirely from the keyboard.

### Written in Rust

A single Rust binary, small and free of configuration daemons.

### Minimal

orilla stays focused on tiling and focus, without bars, panels, or extras.
