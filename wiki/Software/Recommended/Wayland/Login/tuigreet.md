## tuigreet

A TUI greeter for greetd.

tuigreet shows a text-based login screen in the TTY, and hands the session to greetd when you log in.

### Terminal-login, modernized

tuigreet is the classic console login, brought up to date: users can pick a session from a list.

### No GUI

Everything is text.

No image rendering, no compositor needed for the greeter itself.

### Pairs with greetd

tuigreet is one of several greeters for greetd, and the one most in the spirit of this wiki.

It is the login screen of a minimal desktop.
