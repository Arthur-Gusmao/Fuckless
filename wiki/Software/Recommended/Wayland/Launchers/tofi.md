## tofi

A tiny dynamic menu for Wayland.

tofi shows a list of choices and filters them as you type, in the dmenu tradition.

It is smaller still than mew, and a reasonable alternative to it.

### No config file

tofi is configured entirely through command-line flags.

There is nothing to install, generate, or recompile — the options are the program.

### One dependency

tofi links against the wayland client library and little else.

No toolkit, no library stack.

### Fast

Small scope and direct rendering make tofi start instantly.

It is the menu for users who want the smallest moving part.
