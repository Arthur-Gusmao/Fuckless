## mojito

A featherweight status bar for neuswc-based compositors.

mojito is a lemonbar-compatible bar that renders through neuwld.

### Lemonbar-compatible

mojito speaks the lemonbar language, so existing lemonbar scripts and configs carry over.

The bar stays one small program that displays a line of text.

### Featherweight

Like the drink it is named for, mojito is light.

It draws the bar and formats your status; it does not grow a widget engine.

### A neulibs bar

mojito pairs with the neuswc compositors and feeds from tools like neubar.

It is the status bar of the ecosystem.
