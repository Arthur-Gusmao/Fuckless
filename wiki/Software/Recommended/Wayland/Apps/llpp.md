## llpp

A fast PDF viewer based on mupdf.

llpp displays PDF and other document formats with a keyboard-driven interface.

### Fast rendering

llpp renders through mupdf, which is built for speed.

Large documents open and scroll without the weight of a full browser stack.

### Keyboard-first

In the spirit of the rest of this wiki, llpp is driven from the keyboard.

It runs fine on Wayland, where the viewer fits a minimal desktop.

### A minimal document tool

One binary for viewing documents.

It is the closest thing to a suckless PDF viewer that still works well.
