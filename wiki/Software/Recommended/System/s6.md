## s6

A small suite of programs for process supervision and daemon management.

s6 supervises long-running processes, restarts them when they die, and manages their lifecycle without a huge init framework.

### A suite of small tools

s6 ships as separate programs: `s6-supervise`, `s6-svscan`, `s6-log`, `s6-setuidgid`.

Each does one thing, and they compose the way Unix programs do.

### Supervision without the bloat

Services are directories containing a `run` script, and supervision just execs and watches.

There is no declarative language to learn: a service is an executable file and a couple of supporting scripts.

### Logging as a service

`s6-log` runs as a supervised daemon and writes its own logs.

If the logger crashes, the supervisor restarts it, and the service keeps running.

### From the daemontools tradition

s6 follows daemontools' process-supervision idea, done more carefully and more portably.

It is the supervision layer for systems that want control without a monolithic init.
