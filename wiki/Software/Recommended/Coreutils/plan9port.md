## plan9port

Plan 9 from User Space.

plan9port is the full port of the Plan 9 operating system userland to Unix, maintained by Russ Cox.

### The whole userland

It includes the Plan 9 command line tools, window system (`rio`), and drawing libraries.

### Beyond the core

Where 9base is the minimal command line subset, plan9port brings the entire environment: `acme`, `sam`, `rc`, `mk`, `venti`, and more.

### Heavier, still Unix

It is larger than 9base and needs X11 for its graphical tools.

Choose 9base for minimalism, plan9port for the complete Plan 9 experience on Unix.
