## ubase

The suckless linux base utilities.

ubase is the companion to sbase: basic Linux-specific commands, each a tiny standalone program.

### The Linux side

It covers the commands that touch the kernel: `mount`, `umount`, `dmesg`, `login`, `passwd`, `su`, `kill`, and more.

### Same philosophy as sbase

One file per tool, no GNU baggage, nothing you cannot read.

### Together they form a base

sbase provides the generic Unix tools, ubase the Linux ones.

Together they are a minimal, readable replacement for coreutils plus the system utilities.
